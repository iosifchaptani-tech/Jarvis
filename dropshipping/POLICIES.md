# Store policies (ready to paste into Shopify)

**Where they go:** Shopify → Settings → Policies. Paste each section below into the box with the same name.

**Before you paste:**
1. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` (Ctrl+F `[`).
2. Delete the **EU/UK** parts if you only sell to the US.
3. Keep the numbers (days, 30-day returns) the same as your answers in the setup script. The support agent quotes those answers to customers.

These are plain-English templates written for this system: CJ fulfilment, the Claude support agent, Telegram alerts. They are not legal advice. If you register a company or sell in the EU, have a local lawyer or your accountant check them.

**Placeholders:**
| Placeholder | Example |
|---|---|
| `[STORE NAME]` | Glowdesk |
| `[STORE URL]` | glowdesk.com or glowdesk.myshopify.com |
| `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` | the support Gmail from the setup |
| `[LEGAL NAME]` | your full name (sole trader) or your company name |
| `[BUSINESS ADDRESS]` | a real address where you can receive post. A virtual address works if you don't want your home address shown. |
| `[COUNTRY]` / `[STATE/COUNTRY]` | United Kingdom / New York, USA |
| `[DATE]` | the date you publish |

## 1. Refund policy

**Refund and Return Policy**

*Last updated: [DATE]*

We want you to be happy with your order. If something is wrong, email us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** with your order number and we will sort it out.

**Damaged, faulty or wrong item**
- Tell us within **30 days of delivery**. Send a photo of the problem.
- You choose a **free replacement or a full refund**, including shipping.
- You usually don't need to send the item back. If we do need it, we pay for the return.

**Changed your mind**
- You can return unused items in their original packaging within **30 days of delivery**.
- Email us first. Please don't send anything back before we reply, because returns go to a different address from the one on the parcel.
- Return shipping is paid by you, unless the item was faulty or wrong.
- We refund the item price once the return arrives. Original shipping is refunded only if you return the whole order.

**Order never arrived**
If your tracking hasn't changed for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived 10 business days after the latest delivery date we gave you, contact us. We will reship it or give you a full refund.

**Cancelling an order**
Email us as soon as possible. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we cancel it and refund you in full. If it has shipped, follow the return steps above.

**How refunds are paid**
Refunds go back to your original payment method within **5 business days** of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

**Items we can't take back**
Items that are used or damaged by you, or missing parts that you didn't report. This never limits your rights for faulty or wrong items.

**EU and UK customers: your right to cancel** *(delete if you don't sell to the EU/UK)*
- You can cancel your order within **14 days** of receiving it without giving a reason.
- To cancel, use the **cancel/withdraw button** on your order page, or send us a clear statement by email to [SUPPORT EMAIL].
- We refund you, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving your cancellation. We may wait until we receive the item back, or until you show proof you have sent it.
- You pay the direct cost of returning the item.
- You are responsible for any loss in value caused by handling the item more than needed to check it.
- This is on top of your legal rights for faulty goods.

**Contact:** [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We reply within 1-2 business days.

## 3. Privacy policy

**Privacy Policy**

*Last updated: [DATE]*

This policy explains how **[LEGAL NAME]**, trading as **[STORE NAME]** ("we"), collects and uses your personal information when you visit or buy from [STORE URL].

**Who is responsible:** [LEGAL NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

**What we collect**
- **Order details:** name, shipping and billing address, email, phone (if given), what you bought and the price.
- **Payment:** handled by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Messages:** emails you send us and our replies.
- **Browsing data:** device, browser, IP address, pages viewed, collected by Shopify cookies. See Shopify's cookie notice.

**Why we use it**
| Purpose | Legal basis (EU/UK) |
|---|---|
| Process, ship and track your order; handle returns and refunds | Contract |
| Answer your questions | Contract and legitimate interest |
| Prevent fraud and keep the store secure | Legitimate interest |
| Keep tax and accounting records | Legal obligation |
| Marketing emails, only if you opted in | Consent (you can unsubscribe any time) |

**Who we share it with.** We share only what each provider needs to do its job:
- **Shopify:** runs our store and checkout.
- **Payment providers** (Shopify Payments / Stripe, PayPal): take your payment.
- **CJ Dropshipping and delivery carriers:** pack and deliver your order. They get your name, address, phone and order items.
- **Google (Gmail):** hosts our support mailbox.
- **Anthropic (Claude AI):** helps us draft replies to support emails and look up your order status. It sees your message and your order details. Under Anthropic's business terms, your data is not used to train its AI models.
- **Telegram:** sends order and support notifications to our own team account.
- **Cloudflare and our hosting provider:** carry and store data for our order and support system.
- **Authorities:** only when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal information.

**AI and automated decisions.** Some answers to common questions (for example, "where is my order?") may be written with AI help and sent automatically. A person reviews anything about refunds, complaints or problems. We don't make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means only. You can always ask for a person to handle your email.

**International transfers.** Some of these providers are outside your country, for example in the US or China for fulfilment. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

**How long we keep it**
- Order and tax records: [6-7] years, as tax law requires.
- Support emails: up to 2 years.
- Marketing data: until you unsubscribe.

**Your rights.** You can ask to:
- see, correct or delete your data
- restrict or object to how we use it
- get a copy to take elsewhere (portability)
- withdraw consent at any time

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We reply within 30 days.
- **EU/UK:** you can also complain to your data protection authority (UK: the ICO).
- **California and other US states:** you have the right to know, delete and correct your data, and to non-discrimination. We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising [change this if you use Meta/TikTok pixels].

**Children.** Our store is not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

**Security.** We use access controls, encrypted connections and strong passwords. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

**Changes.** We update this page when things change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
